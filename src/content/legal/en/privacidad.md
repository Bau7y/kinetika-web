This policy explains how Kinetika handles the personal data it receives through this site, under Costa Rica’s Law No. 8968 on the Protection of Individuals with regard to the Processing of their Personal Data.

## Data controller

Kinetika, the trade name of a software development venture based in San Carlos, Alajuela, Costa Rica. For any request about your data you can write to us at [kinetikacr.sc@gmail.com](mailto:kinetikacr.sc@gmail.com), use the [contact form](/en/contact) or reply to the email we contacted you from. If you need the full identification of the person responsible in order to exercise your rights, ask for it through those same channels.

Form messages are kept in our email inbox, which serves as the record of requests.

## What data we collect

- **Quote:** type of business, what you want to solve, your email address and, if you write one, a message.
- **Waitlist:** your email address and, if you provide it, the name of your firm.
- **Technical data:** the minimum needed to prevent automated or abusive form submissions. It is not used to identify you.
- **Visit statistics:** measured with Vercel Web Analytics and counted in aggregate: page views, referring page, country, device type and browser. No cookies are stored, and people are neither identified nor followed across other sites.

The project demos run only in your browser: whatever you type in them never reaches us.

## Cookies

This site does not use its own or third-party cookies, nor any advertising tools. The visit measurement described above does not use cookies either. Your browser stores only two preferences for your convenience: the language you chose and whether you already saw the intro animation. They never reach us and you can delete them by clearing the site’s data.

## What we use it for

- Answering your request and sending you a proposal or a demo.
- Letting you know when a waitlisted project becomes available.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or share it with third parties beyond the services that run the site.

Before sending a form you tick the box accepting this policy. That consent is recorded in the message we receive.

## Who else is involved

To run the site we use web hosting, email delivery and anti-spam verification providers. They only process the data on our behalf and for those purposes. Some store information on servers outside Costa Rica.

## How long we keep it

As long as needed to handle your request. Waitlist data is kept until the project is available or until you ask to leave the list. After that we delete it, unless a law requires us to keep it.

## Your rights

At any time you can ask to **access**, **correct** or **delete** your data, **object** to its use and **withdraw your consent**. It is free of charge and we resolve it within five business days of receiving the request. If you believe your rights were not respected, you can turn to Costa Rica’s data protection agency (PRODHAB).

Providing your data is voluntary, but without it we cannot answer your request.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date.
