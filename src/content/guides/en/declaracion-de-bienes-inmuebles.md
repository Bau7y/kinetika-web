## Who has to declare

Costa Rica’s Law No. 7509, the Real Estate Tax Law, requires property owners to declare its value to the municipality of the canton where the property is located, **at least every five years** (article 16).

## What you need

In San Carlos the declaration is filed with the municipality’s Real Estate Section (Sección de Bienes Inmuebles) with:

1. **The official declaration form**, filled in with the actual details of the land and the buildings.
2. **Your ID document.**

Before filling it in, have the property details at hand: property number (número de finca), survey plan and a description of what is built.

## Why it pays to keep it up to date

- If the declaration is not filed, the municipality may **value the property on its own**, that is, appraise it without your declaration (article 17).
- An up-to-date declaration is a requirement to apply for the **single-property exemption**.
- Declaring before the municipality appraises the property lets you describe it as it is.
